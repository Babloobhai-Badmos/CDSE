Report an Accessibility Issue

Help CDSE keep NLU Jodhpur accessible. Tell us about ramps, railings, surfaces, signage, restrooms, digital resources, or anything else that needs attention.

Spotted a barrier on campus? Tell us what it is (ramp, railing, surface, signage, restroom, digital resource, golf-cart service or other), where it is, and how urgent it feels. Your name and email are optional, but leaving them lets us follow up. High-urgency reports are flagged to the audit team straight away.

Form not loading? Open it in a new tab, or email us directly at cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in.

For urgent safety concerns, please also contact campus security directly in addition to filing this report.

What you send is used only to respond to your message or application. We do not share it outside CDSE, and we do not use these forms to build mailing lists. Ask us to delete your submission at any time by emailing cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in.

Email us at cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in
