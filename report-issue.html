Report an Accessibility Issue

Help CDSE keep NLU Jodhpur accessible. Tell us about ramps, railings, surfaces, signage, restrooms, digital resources, or anything else that needs attention.

Setup needed: this embeds a Google Form. Create a form with fields for Name (optional), Email (optional), Type of issue (Ramp / Railing / Surface / Signage / Restroom / Digital resource / Golf-cart service / Other), Location on campus, Description, and Urgency (Low / Medium / High). Then replace FORM_ID_ACCESSIBILITY_REPORT below with the real form ID. Apps Script can auto-notify the audit team by email the moment a "High" urgency report comes in — see docs/google-forms-setup.md.

Form not loading? Open it in a new tab, or email us directly at cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in.

For urgent safety concerns, please also contact campus security directly in addition to filing this report.

Email us at cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in
