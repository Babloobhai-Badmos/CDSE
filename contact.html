Contact & Feedback

Questions, feedback, media enquiries, or general messages — we'd like to hear from you.

Send us a message

Have a question, feedback on our work, or an idea for collaboration? Tell us who you are, what it is about (general enquiry, feedback on CDSE's work, feedback on this website, media or press, or partnership), and write your message. We read every note and aim to reply within a week.

What you send is used only to respond to your message or application. We do not share it outside CDSE, and we do not use these forms to build mailing lists. Ask us to delete your submission at any time by emailing cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in.
