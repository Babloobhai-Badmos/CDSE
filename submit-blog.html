Submit a Blog

The Centre for Disability Studies and Empowerment invites original submissions for its blog, which seeks to foster critical discourse at the intersection of law and disability.

Contributors are requested to adhere to the following guidelines to ensure uniformity, academic quality, and accessibility.

Scope and Subject Matter

  • Submissions must relate to issues at the intersection of law and disability.
  • Interdisciplinary approaches drawing from sociology, psychology, economics, public policy, technology, medicine, or other related fields are encouraged.
  • The blog aims to promote nuanced, well-researched insights that advance conversations on disability rights and empowerment.

Word Limit and Authorship

  • Main text: 1000–1500 words, plus a brief abstract of about 100 words.
  • Individual or co-authorship of up to two authors is permitted.
  • Submissions must be original and not under review elsewhere pending the completion of the review process.

Citation and Referencing

  • All sources must be hyperlinked within the text (avoid footnotes unless absolutely necessary).
  • Similarity must not exceed 10%.
  • Use of AI-generated content is strictly prohibited.

Formatting Guidelines

  • Font: Times New Roman, size 12
  • Line spacing: 1.5, alignment: justified
  • Headings: bold and underlined
  • File name format: CDSE_blogsubmission_Title of the blog

Submit by email

Submission Procedure and Review Process

  • Submissions should be emailed in .doc or .docx format to cdse.blog@nlujodhpur.ac.in.
  • Email subject line must read: “CDSE Blog Submission – [Title of the Blog]”
  • Submissions will be accepted on a rolling basis.
  • The Editorial Board will conduct a preliminary screening followed by a detailed review.
  • The decision of the Editorial Board shall be final and binding.
Email Your Submission

Prefer a form?

Submit via Google Form

Prefer not to email? Send your piece through the form instead. You will need your blog title, author name(s), email, a short abstract (about 100 words) and your .doc/.docx manuscript, named CDSE_blogsubmission_Title of the blog. You will also confirm that the work is original, under 10% similarity, and free of AI-generated content.

Form not loading? Open it in a new tab, or email your submission directly to cdse.blog@nlujodhpur.ac.in.

What you send is used only to respond to your message or application. We do not share it outside CDSE, and we do not use these forms to build mailing lists. Ask us to delete your submission at any time by emailing cdse@nlujodhpur.ac.in.
