Our belief

Disability justice is inseparable from social justice

The Centre for Disability Studies and Empowerment (CDSE) is a student-run, interdisciplinary initiative at National Law University, Jodhpur, dedicated to advancing disability rights, inclusive policy, and accessible design in India. Our work is grounded in the belief that disability justice is inseparable from social justice.

Who we are

CDSE brings together scholars, practitioners, disability advocates, and Millennium Fellowship-trained student leaders committed to promoting equal participation, legal empowerment, and socio-economic inclusion of persons with disabilities.

The Centre engages in research, policy analysis, accessibility audits, legal literacy programmes, and community outreach, with a focus on disability law, reasonable accommodation, inclusive education, and rights-based approaches to assistive technologies.

Our members collaborate with national and international organisations, contribute to public discourse, and work towards strengthening India's disability rights framework through evidence-based advocacy and intersectional scholarship.

What we do

Our areas of work

Research

Scholarly research into disability law, rights, and inclusive policy design in India.

Policy Analysis

Analysing legislation and institutional policy through a disability-rights lens, with a focus on reasonable accommodation.

Accessibility Audits

Assessing physical and digital infrastructure for accessibility, and pushing for concrete remediation.

Legal Literacy & Community Outreach

Programmes that build legal literacy and community awareness around disability rights.

Milestones

Our journey so far

  • October 2025

    Launched a golf-cart mobility service so students requiring assisted movement can request support through the main gate.

  • December 2025

    NLU Jodhpur and the Rajiv K. Luthra Foundation signed an MoU to establish the Rajiv K. Luthra – NLUJ Accessibility Lab.

  • May 2026

    The Rajiv K. Luthra – NLUJ Accessibility Lab was inaugurated by students with special abilities, in the presence of Vice Chancellor Prof. (Dr.) Harpreet Kaur, Registrar Dr. Sunita Pankaj, and representatives of the Luthra Foundation.

  • June 2026

    Opened a rolling call for blog submissions on disability rights, accessibility, and inclusive policy.
