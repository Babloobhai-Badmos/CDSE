Our Projects

From physical infrastructure to institutional facilities, CDSE turns audits and advocacy into concrete change on campus.

Flagship facility

Rajiv K. Luthra – NLUJ Accessibility Lab

The first dedicated accessibility facility of its kind at National Law University, Jodhpur, administered under CDSE. Established through a Memorandum of Understanding signed between the Rajiv K. Luthra Foundation and NLUJ in December 2025, the Lab was formally inaugurated in May 2026 — fittingly, by students with special abilities.

The state-of-the-art facility supports students with visual, auditory, locomotor, learning, and other disabilities through both academic and infrastructural assistance. It houses:

  • Screen readers
  • Magnifiers
  • Accessible digital repositories
  • Adaptive workstations

The inauguration ceremony was attended by Vice Chancellor Prof. (Dr.) Harpreet Kaur, Registrar Dr. Sunita Pankaj, and representatives of the Rajiv K. Luthra Foundation. The Lab bears the name of the late Shri Rajiv K. Luthra, one of India's most eminent corporate lawyers and a lifelong advocate of equal opportunity and professional excellence. The partnership also includes internship opportunities each year at Luthra & Luthra Law Offices for two students with disabilities from NLUJ.

Campus mobility

Golf-Cart Mobility Service

Launched in October 2025, this service enhances campus accessibility for students with disabilities. Any student who requires assisted movement can request the golf cart through the main gate, ensuring dignified, flexible assistance around campus.

Infrastructure

Campus Accessibility Audits

CDSE conducts accessibility audits of the NLUJ campus and translates findings into recommendations for the University. Recommendations have included the construction of properly graded ramps, installation of double railings, and use of anti-slip surfacing to ensure safe and independent movement. Following these observations, the University moved quickly to construct several new PWD-friendly ramps across campus, prioritising the most critical access points first.
