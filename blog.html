Call for blogs

Write for the CDSE Blog

CDSE invites blog submissions on disability rights, accessibility, inclusive policy, and legal empowerment. Submissions are accepted on a rolling basis.

Format

  • Main text: 1000–1500 words
  • Abstract: approximately 100 words
  • Individual or co-authorship (up to two authors)

Content requirements

  • Original submissions only — not under review elsewhere
  • All sources hyperlinked within the text
  • Avoid footnotes unless absolutely necessary
  • Similarity/plagiarism must not exceed 10%
  • AI-generated content is strictly prohibited

Review process: submissions are accepted on a rolling basis. The Editorial Board conducts a preliminary screening followed by a detailed peer review.
