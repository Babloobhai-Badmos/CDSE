What we do

Research, advocacy, and accessible design — together

CDSE brings together scholars, practitioners, disability advocates, and Millennium Fellowship-trained student leaders committed to promoting equal participation, legal empowerment, and socio-economic inclusion of persons with disabilities. Our work spans research, policy analysis, accessibility audits, legal literacy programmes, and community outreach, with a focus on disability law and reasonable accommodation.

Research & Policy

Research & Policy Analysis

Studying disability law, reasonable accommodation, and inclusive policy design in the Indian context.

Campus

Accessibility Audits

Assessing campus infrastructure and recommending fixes — ramps, railings, signage, and more — to make NLUJ genuinely accessible.

Outreach

Legal Literacy & Outreach

Running legal literacy programmes and community outreach to advance equal participation for persons with disabilities.

Featured project

Rajiv K. Luthra – NLUJ Accessibility Lab

The first dedicated accessibility facility of its kind at NLU Jodhpur, established through an MoU with the Rajiv K. Luthra Foundation and inaugurated by students with special abilities. The Lab houses screen readers, magnifiers, accessible digital repositories, and adaptive workstations supporting students with visual, auditory, locomotor, learning, and other disabilities.
