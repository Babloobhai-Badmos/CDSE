Centre for Disability Studies & Empowerment

The Centre for Disability Studies and Empowerment (CDSE) is an academic and research-driven centre committed to advancing disability justice, inclusive policy development, and empowerment-based scholarship.

Get Involved Learn About CDSE
“There is no greater disability in society than the inability to see a person as more.”

Robert M. Hensel

What we do

Research, advocacy, and accessible design — together

CDSE brings together scholars, practitioners, disability advocates, and Millennium Fellowship-trained student leaders committed to promoting equal participation, legal empowerment, and socio-economic inclusion of persons with disabilities.

Research & Policy

Research & Policy Analysis

Studying disability law, reasonable accommodation, and inclusive policy design in the Indian context.

Campus

Accessibility Audits

Assessing campus infrastructure and recommending fixes — ramps, railings, signage, and more.

Outreach

Legal Literacy & Outreach

Running legal literacy programmes and community outreach to advance equal participation.

Featured project

Rajiv K. Luthra – NLUJ Accessibility Lab

The first dedicated accessibility facility of its kind at NLU Jodhpur, established through an MoU with the Rajiv K. Luthra Foundation and inaugurated by students with special abilities.
